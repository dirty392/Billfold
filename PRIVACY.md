# Privacy

Billfold keeps your information on your phone and nowhere else.

- **No internet access.** The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything online.
- **No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.**
- **Your data stays on the device**, inside the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Files only leave when you choose.** Backups and CSV exports are saved where you pick, and it's up to you where they go next.

Billfold asks for two permissions:
- **Notifications**, to remind you before bills are due (only if you turn reminders on).
- **Run at startup**, so reminders still go off after your phone restarts.
