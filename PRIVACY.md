# Privacy

Billfold keeps your information on your phone and nowhere else.

- **Offline unless you turn on live metal prices.** Billfold goes online for one thing only, and only if you switch on *Settings → Metal prices → Check live prices*. It is off until you turn it on.
  - When it's on, the app asks [gold-api.com](https://gold-api.com) for the current price of gold, silver, platinum, palladium and copper in your chosen currency. The request contains only the metal and currency codes. Nothing about you, your holdings or your money is sent.
  - The app's screens themselves are blocked from loading anything from the internet; only this one price check can reach it.
  - Turn the switch off and Billfold never goes online.
- **No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.**
- **Your data stays on the device**, inside the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Files only leave when you choose.** Backups and CSV exports are saved where you pick, and it's up to you where they go next.

Billfold asks for three permissions:
- **Notifications**, to remind you before bills are due (only if you turn reminders on).
- **Run at startup**, so reminders still go off after your phone restarts.
- **Internet**, used only for live metal prices when you turn them on.
